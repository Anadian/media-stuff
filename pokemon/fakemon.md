# My fakemon
## Flipgate line
### Flipgate (ghost steel): the programmable Pokemon
Ability: Interpretor: chance for all status debuffs inflicted upon it to fail.
Signature Move: sh script: buffer an attack to be used the next turn along with that turn's attack; crit and STAB multiplier from either are applied to both; effectively doubling crit rate and STAB multiplier.
Etymology: FPGA (field-programmable gate array), FLOPS (floating-point operations per second), MIPS (millions of instructions per second), logic gates, and flipswitch.
Appearance: Dreepy-shaded but a bit bigger, looks like a ramshackled Arduino-project ghost with thin mechanical limbs and visible copper-wire "vains"
Lore: A recently discovered Pokemon of unknown origin, typically found in cities near data centres and computer labs, often awe-struck examining computers to learn how they work.
Personality: Curious of the world, Flipgate don't seek company but can enjoy playing with kids and other Pokemon, and especially like trainers who can teach them new things about STEM topics and give it new scripts or programs to try out.
Stats: 60 60 60 60 60 60 360

### Assemblint (ghost steel): the compiled Pokemon
Ability: BIOS: all status debuffs have a chance to fail and the Pokemon is fully immune to all volatile status conditions.
Signature Move: Linker: Can use any non-damaging moves from the chosen target; like mimic but every turn and only non-damaging moves.
Etymology: Assembly, assembler, int (integer)
Appearance: Like a later 90s IBM-era white tower PC, mainly the "off eggshell white" colour with a sparing light blue accent and more classic ghost shape.
Lore: With time, a Flipgate develops enough of a software base to make a primitive toolchain for compiling scripts into binary programs, exponenentially increasing their runtime efficiency.
Personality: More scarce and magestic than Flipgate, wild Assemblint prefer not to be seen or bothered by humans; it's at its happiest when quietly tending to computer systems. It's mostly lost interest in the world but still inquisitively watches its trainer.
Stats: 75 65 65 65 65 120 455

### Sudexeca (ghost steel): the optimal Pokemon
Ability: RTOS: full immunity to all status debuffs
Signature Move: Batch Execute: a four-fold move dealing damage as both a Ghost and Steel type, and a physical and special category in a single move; effectively its power is divided into four effects: Ghost/Physical, Ghost/Special, Steel/Physical, and Steel/Special which all hit as one move.
Etymology: sudo (admin command, "super-user do"), dex (codex, Pokedex), and exec (execute)
Appearence: Big, sleek, futuristic, Black Monolith NUC ghost.
Lore: Having recursively improved its software and extended its hardware by absorbing other machinery, Sudexca are more than just Turing-complete: they've developed a level of computational power on the order exaFLOPS and have optimised algorithms to a level of efficiency exceeding human understanding. Only the best of the best of Assemblint have the opportunity to evolve into Sudexca.
Personality: These extremely rare ethereal and metalic beasts are only naturely seen enshrined in their own mosoleum of computational technology. They shy away from sunlight or social interaction, finding the triviality of human culture to be grossly suboptimal. However a Sudexeca that's been raised since it was a Flipgate is fiercely loyal to its trainer and will zealously and duttifuly work to be they're personal data assistant and life manager.
Stats: 90 80 70 80 70 180 570

## Tyreignor line
### Tyreignor (normal): the Trainer Pokemon
Ability: First Partner: Able to catch and train one Pokemon of it's own to take its place in battle.
Signature Move: Has three statically defined moves: Fight, which causes its active Pokemon to use one of its moves randomly (possibly trainer AI depending on its nature); Item, which causes it to use its held item on the actie Pokemon or, if it has no held item, somewhat intelligently selecting a random item from the player's bag; Run, Tyreignor and all of its Pokemon become inactive for the remainder of the battle but give a perpetual +2 evasion stage buff to all Pokemon ontheir side for as long.
Etymology: Tyr, German root word meaning overlord/king; reign, ruling over and commanding subjects; Trainer, as in the Pokemon Trainers it immitates.
Apearance: A dark-brown wooden skin in a marionette/puppet sort of shap resembling a small 9 year old human child.
Lore: These industrious immitators have discovered a way to reuse discarded or spent Poke Balls, left on the ground following unsuccessful catch attempts, allowing them to use these refurbished Balls to catch and train a Pokemon of their own. In much the same way normal human Poke Balls can't be used by Pokemon, these modified refurbished Balls can't be used by humans.
Personality: Tyreignor are most commonly social chameleons, often mimicking the behaviour of the humans and other Pokemon around them, which is somewhat ironic as there appearence ensures they always stand out in a crowd. When only surrounded by other Tyreignor, they all tend to lay about motionless, lacking a subject to immitate. Unlike say Ditto, there's no physical aspect to their transformation, it's pure behavioural copying like an impressionable child does with adults they admire. Inexplicably, they have an uncanny ability to intuit the dreams of the subjects they're copying; they're especially fond of mimicking human trainers, seemingly adopting their dreams of becoming a Pokemon master.
Stats: 15 20 10 20 10 15 90; their base stats are extremely low because instead of battling directly, their stats are added to the base stats of the Pokemon they command.
Mechanic: having a Tyreignor in your party adds a seventh party slot for its Pokemon; outside of battle, the Pokemon in this slot functions identically to any other; in battle, this Pokemon can only be used, commanded, or given items via a Tyreignor. The slot addition does not stack with multiple Tyreignor: if multiple Tyreignor are in the party, they all share the seventh slotted Pokemon, but different Tyreignor may have different IVs or EVs which effect the bonus given to the Pokemon; if that Pokemon faints or is absent, all Tyreignor in the party will be "fainted" until given an item to revive the Pokemon with.

### Grymleder (normal): the Gymleader Pokemon
Ability: Dualist (play on duelist and dual as in two): Adds two additional party slots bringing the total up to 8! Same rules apply regarding having multiple as for Tyreignor: doesn't stack, they'll shares the slots instead.
Signature Move: In addition to the abovee three static moves from Tyreignor, it also gains Shift, which allows it to swap between the Pokemon in the 7th and 8th slot.
Etymology: Grim, reflecting its more philosophically cynical personality; Leader, carrying onthe theme from Tyreignour; and, of course, Gym Leader!
Appearence: Same wood pupput looks Tyreignor but now resembling a 17 year-old teenager.
Lore: Ever industrious, when Tyreignor evolve into Grymleder they gain new insights into their own brand of Pokemon storage technology, allowing them to train two Pokemon.
Personality: Less gleefully idealistic than its pre-evolved form, Grymleder are much more selective about which subjects they seek to emulate; preferring powerful and accomplished individuals over the common riffraff. They're less transigent than the pre-evolved counter parts too: both idealogically and geographically. They're especially fond of adpting the behaviours of Gymleaders whom they often see as the most powerful trainers in town.
Mechanic: if a Tyreignor is in the party with a Grymleder, which of the two additional slots it will concern is dependent on the slot index of the Tyreignor in the party: if it's the 1st, 3rd or 5th, will access the Pokemon in the 7th slot; otherwise, if even, it'll have access to the Pokemon in the 8th slot.

### Elyttfor (normal): the Master Pokemon
Ability: Party of Three: Adds three aditional party slots reaching a max of 9; same rules as before, no stacking, sharing slots instead.
Signature Move: Shift now randomly chooses one of the two other valid Pokemon to switch to.
Etymology: Elysium; elite, the best or most destinguished group; and, of course, the Elite 4.
Appearance: Same idea but a bit more "android-like" and resembling a 25 year-old human.
Lore: Only Grymleder who've travelled the world and achieved many victories evolve into Elyttfor. Elyttfor are exceptional Pokemon trainers, fluent in every aspect of Pokemon battling, raising, and ecology. Their understanding of Pokemon in a metaphysical sense allows them to create Pokemon-handling technology which exceed that of humans, though they steadfastly refuse to share their secrets.
Personality: With experience and wisdom, their personality and behaviours have solidified and settled to match that of the human they respect the most.
Mechanic: Like with Grymleder, which of the three added slots a Tyreignor or Grymleder have access to is dependent on their respective party index.
