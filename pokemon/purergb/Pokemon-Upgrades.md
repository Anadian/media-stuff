# Pokemon Upgrades

Pokemon | Location(s) | Quest Steps | Reward
--- | --- | --- | ---
Onix | Prospector’s cave on Route 8 | Bring 10 Repels to the prospecter in the cave on Route 8 | Buffed stats; Ground type becomes “Crystal” type; gains resistance to Ice, Water, and Grass.
Magmar | West side of Cinnabar Volcano | Use a Magmar on the magma bath after completing the story quest in Cinnabar Volcano. | Magmar gains “Magma” type; gains immunity to Water and Fire; its Ground-type moves deal bonus damage.
Weezing | Basement of Team Rocket Hideout in Saffron City | Use a Weezing on the exposed pipe. | Weezing gains “Floating” type; becomes immune to Ground.
Magneton | Very end of the Power Plant | Use a Magneton on the large magnet. | Magneton gains “Floating” type; becomes immune to Ground.
Marowak | Pokemon Tower B1F | Catch the grieving Marowak ghost in the Tower during the main story and, after all gyms, bring it to the Dark Channeler in Tower B1F; defeat several powerful mutated Pokemon battles to ultimately lay the Marowak to rest. | Lose the Marowak (let it move on) but gain a buffed Cubone with Bonenerang.
Dragonair | Safari Zone (Sara), Fuchsia City (Erik), Route 19 (floating house), Seafoam Islands on Route 20 | Reunite Sara and Erik in Fuchsia; bring a high-level (45 or higher) Dragonair to their house on Route 19; defeat a level 45 Cloyster with only Dragonair. | Dragonair gains Ice type; gets buffed stats; learns Ice Beam; can no longer evole into a Dragonite.
Mewtwo | TopSecretLab (basement of Pokemon Mansion in Cinnabar) | Unlock the lab by using the TopSecretKey on the plant in Mansion B1F; enter the musical “password” (1, 0, 6, 5, 2, 3, 4, 5); press the floor button near the machine; use Mewtwo on the machine. | Mewtwo gains armor but its type is unchanged.
Gengar | Lavender Town: Cubone house (purification); Tower B1F (cursing) | Bring a Gengar to the Dark Channeler in Tower B1F after the Marowak quest to have her “curse” the Gengar; after which, taking said cursed Gengar to the Light Channeler in Lavender Town’s Cubone House can have it “purified.” | Cursing the Gengar turns its sprite back into a Haunter and then purifying it returns it to the Gengar battle sprite; this is purely cosmetic and doesn’t effect the stats so it’s not really an upgrade quest.
