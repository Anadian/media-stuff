Some suggestions for improving IV3:

    1. Please switch the buttons for going prone (D-pad Down) and making an audio distraction (Gamepad A): going prone is WAY more important and frequent than that useless shout button and should be a face button; yes, you can technically swap them in the options but in IV2 at least, doing so would break the menu/UI controls (which you should be able to map independently of the gameplay controls).
    While we're at it, can we make the shout button actually do something: in my 40 hours of IV2 and 1.5 hours of the IV3 playtest, I've not once been able to get an NPC to react to my character shouting "nice cock," "free gun over here" or whatever; I've tried it while standing right fucking next to the guy and still no reaction; they react to thrown cans, empty magazines, bashed doors, and footsteps but shouting at them never does anything, and no I don't want to use a real microphone but even holding down the button and screaming into the mic doesn't work.
    An option or separate volume slider for the "electrical hum" and broken bulb flickering sounds: I enjoy a slow, methodical stealth experience but I'm sick of hearing those sounds.
    Removing the ability to cycle through points of interaction feels like a regression: I get that you want it to be based on aiming now, but it's far too flimsy and unreliable.
    The least of an actual issue per se but I'm disappointed by the lack of any new features or improvements over IV2: aside from the aforementioned removal of the ability to cycle through interactables, the only other additions I noticed in my playtime are the text log in dialogue and the "eavesdropping" mechanic, both of which are nice but don't meaningfully enhance the gameplay. This just felt like more IV2 which, on the one hand, is great: I loved IV2, played through multiple times to get the "duality of man" achievement and get every blueprint/equipment/mod; but on the other, so far I haven't seen any reason this warrants being its own game when it easily just could've been a DLC campaign for IV2.
